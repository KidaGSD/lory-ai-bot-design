Task switch a move, not a state

When something else takes the Bar, a running task drips off as its hat and flies to the lane, where it keeps working as a small orb. When it comes back, the app it made room for moves to the lane, so there is only ever one LORY.

Design decisions

What we chose, and why, so the build keeps to it.

The hat is LORY’s attention

One detail carries every meaning. It began as the drop on the first, pear-shaped mark; as a hat it points where LORY looks, walks the edge as progress and falls off as the result. There is no spinner, ring or star next to it.

Soft-bean eyes, not tall ovals

Tall glowing ovals read as the Grok bot. Short, round beans tilted in read as a friendly creature, and they still hold at 22 px.

The light says who wrote it; the icon says who it goes to

A reply LORY drafts for Morgan is glass lit from inside, with no icon: it goes to Morgan. Only words that go to LORY carry the mark. Key points LORY wrote are lit the same way.

No circle, and a small light on other icons

In the Bar and on the lane the creature stands on its own. On someone else’s photo or app it becomes a small light on the corner, so it marks LORY without covering the icon.
